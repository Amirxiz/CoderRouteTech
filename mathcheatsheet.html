1. Sigma Symbol ()

The Symbol () is a mathematical symbol used to represent summation, which is the process of adding a sequence of numbers together. Instead of writing every addition separately, Sigma notation provides a shorter and more organized way to represent the calculation.

    • Indefinite Integral: Represents a family of antiderivatives and includes a constant of integration, C.
